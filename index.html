Questions

Is Teneo medical advice?

No. Teneo is a training and nutrition tracker. It never tells you how much medication to take. It records what you and your prescriber decide, and helps you train and eat around it. Talk to your doctor before changing your medication, diet or exercise.

Which medications does it work with?

Teneo is built for weekly GLP-1 injections, including semaglutide and tirzepatide, brand-name or compounded. You log your dose and shot day, and the plan follows your week.

Do I need a gym?

No. Tell Teneo what you have, from a full gym to nothing at all, and it builds around it. There are bodyweight plans for training at home.

Do I need an Apple Watch or a smart scale?

No. Teneo works on its own. If you have an Apple Watch, an Oura ring or a smart scale, it picks up their data through Apple Health.

What happens to my data if I change phones?

Your logs live on your iPhone. Before you switch, export them from Profile in one tap. Optional backup and sync is on the way.
